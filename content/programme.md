The full programme will be announced in spring 2027. The outline below gives an indication of the conference structure.

## Wednesday 11 June — Pre-conference Field Excursion

An optional full-day excursion to the Hel Peninsula will be offered to registered delegates. The Hel Peninsula is a narrow sand spit extending 35 km into the Baltic Sea and hosts breeding colonies of common tern and sandwich tern, as well as important staging populations of waders and wildfowl. Transport by coach from the conference venue departs at 07:00 and returns by 19:00. Places are limited and must be booked separately during registration.

## Poster Sessions

Poster boards will be available throughout the conference. Presenters are asked to be present at their posters during the designated sessions. Posters should be prepared in A0 portrait format. Printing facilities are available at the conference venue at additional cost; please contact the organising committee in advance if you wish to use this service.

## Workshops

A limited number of pre-registered workshops will be held in parallel with the main programme. Details and registration links will be published when the full programme is announced. Workshop places are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.
